Our story

A little juice bar with a big favourite-choice reputation

Tucked along Ginnaliya Road in the heart of Urubokka, Café R2 has become the go-to spot for families, students and travellers passing through the Deniyaya–Embilipitiya route. Every glass is made fresh to order, from real fruit and quality ingredients — no shortcuts.

Whether you're stopping by for a quick mango lassi on your way to Ginnaliya, celebrating a birthday, or just cooling down with a mojito after a long day, our team treats every customer like a regular.
